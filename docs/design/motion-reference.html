1. Tabs and «+»
The active pill slides between tabs. «+» does NOT rotate: it shrinks and fades (scale .6, opacity 0) while the menu grows out of its bottom-right corner (scale .24 → 1, soft spring). Menu: card bg, card-2 border, radius 28, padding 8. First item is highlighted with a card-2 pill. Every item has a lime icon. The lime pill «Закрыть» closes it. In the real app the tab bar shows the active tab as icon + label (see docs/design/Main.dc.html).
