## Visible differences vs reference (ApplePay.dc.html / motion #2)
1. Backdrop dimming: the app dims the whole Главная (about 60% black) behind the island. The reference has no dim. The result is muddy olive cards and a darker grey header.
2. Start pill: at about 100 ms the island is a wide black rounded rectangle (about 360x190 px) floating at the top centre, over the greeting and the bell. The reference pill is a small capsule inside its own box. The growth looks like a blob rather than a pill. In the collapse frame (300 ms) the "Поделено" pill sits over the header and hides the bell.
3. Top offset: the open island is inset from the top by the safe area, sitting over the hero card and covering «Баланс». The reference is a standalone card at the top with the lock-screen notification below it.
4. Open card matches the reference closely (cart disc, merchant, grey «Что купил?», lime «Продукты» chip, dark chips, ghost «Написать…», lime «Общее · по N ₸», «Моё»). The card is wider and taller than the reference at the same phone width, and the radius is larger.
5. The reference's «Написать…» state is a lime-bordered input with a round ↑ button inside the notification. In the app it is an input inside the island. Spec-acceptable, but it differs.
6. Time label: no «Magnum · 6 000 ₸» title in the app, because no notification UI exists in-app (SW only).
7. Description without a merchant shows «Покупка», and a personal row in the payer's list is labelled «все скинули» (copy mismatch for a personal purchase). Not matched against the reference.
8. Main: the Главная reference has no «Ждут решения». In the app the section sits BELOW «Долги» and under the TabBar on the first screen (only the heading peeks out, darkened behind the bar). The spec says "top, above Подтверди оплату". The section is buried and not at the top.
9. /settings/shortcut: capsule language is consistent (back pill, outline capsule fields, lime numbered discs, lime warning). The token is cut off with "…", so the user cannot verify what they copy. The page does not look like Invite.dc.html's code block.

## Prioritized fixes
1. Move «Ждут решения» to the top of Главная (above Долги and the quick actions or right under the hero), as the spec says. Right now a pending capture is invisible without scrolling.
2. Remove (or drastically lighten) the dim backdrop, or limit it to a hairline scrim, to match motion #2. Keep a transparent tap-outside layer.
3. Fix the island origin geometry: start as a small black capsule (about 120-160 px wide) at the top centre, grow in width/height with content fading late, and collapse to a compact "✓ Поделено" pill that does not cover the header controls. Match the reference radius and width (368 px was claimed; the measured card is wider/taller on iPhone 15).
4. «все скинули» on personal purchases and the «Покупка» fallback copy: review the copy (a personal purchase should not read as shared).
5. Show the full token (wrap, monospace) or a reveal toggle so it can be verified before copying.
6. Side-by-side screenshots in the repo are generated by the Generator and should be re-run after fixes 1-3.
